## Findings

### FU-19: the relay bridge keeps forwarding a stream's buffered data after the connector's connection closed

- **Observed (R3):** the relay removed the connector from its registry 0.6 s after the stop, but each bridged stream kept forwarding for up to 49–52 s at 25 KB/s. It ended only at `pipe Connector to Client: read: connection lost`, then forwarded `RLAB`.
- **Inferred cause:** the same quinn behaviour as FU-16's R1. After the peer's `CONNECTION_CLOSE`, `RecvStream::read` keeps returning the data buffered for the stream (up to its receive window, 1.25 MB by default) and reports the loss only once drained. The client was fixed for this in FU-16 (`WatchedStream`); the relay's bridge (`relay/src/session.rs`) was not.
- **Impact:** for relayed flows of a slow reader, the app keeps receiving up to ~1.25 MB of the stopped connector's data, until the relay buffer drains (≈50 s at 25 KB/s), before the error. At typical rates the tail is seconds (300 KB/s: +7–8 s).
- **Fix direction:**
  - in the relay bridge, watch the connector connection (`closed()`) next to each stream read;
  - once it is closed, reset the client-side stream at once with the forwarded code instead of draining the buffer;
  - a relay change; no connector, client or proto change.
