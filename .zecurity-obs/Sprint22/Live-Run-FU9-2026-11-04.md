## L7: isolation runs

**Method:**
- Four member removals during `curl --limit-rate 300K`, two on the shield route (FU-9 code in the path) and two on a connector-route resource on inkyank (no FU-9 code).
- curl's bytes and this PC's UDP receive counter (`/proc/net/snmp` InDatagrams; background rate about 1.5 datagrams/s) were sampled every 10 ms.
- `tcpdump` is not installed here.

| Run | Route | curl ended after the cut | Tail after the cut | Last UDP burst from the connector, relative to the cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | connector (no FU-9) | 3.09 s | 1.10 MB | +0.31 s |
| 1 | shield (FU-9) | 3.23 s | 0.98 MB | −0.58 s |
| 2 | connector (no FU-9) | 3.08 s | 1.10 MB | −0.72 s |
| 2 | shield (FU-9) | 2.28 s | 0.85 MB | −0.48 s |

**Hop timeline, shield run 2** (cut at 11:24:38.120, mutation returned .128):
1. The controller pushes the new ACL (v21) at 11:24:38. Its log has whole seconds only.
2. The connector cancels both sessions at .111 by inkyank's clock, which is not synchronised with this PC and appears 10–20 ms behind.
3. The connector unregisters the session at .1117. There is no "device stream not aborted" warning, so the abort path ran.
4. No connector traffic reaches this PC after the cut (UDP counter).
5. The client gets FU-8's change hint at .125, syncs the snapshot at .130, and hot-removes the resources at .148.
6. curl's last byte arrives at +2.25 s, then error rc=56 at +2.28 s.

**Not observable at info level:** when the client saw the QUIC reset, and how many bytes the resource accepted.

**Conclusion:**
- The tail is the same with and without FU-9's code.
- The connector aborts at once, and nothing more arrives over the network after the cut.
- So the 2–3 s / ~1 MB is data the client was already holding, handed to the app at its read rate before the reset.
- FU-15 measured 0.52 s / 0.18 MB on 2026-10-31, before FU-8 (immediate hot-remove) merged. The suspected cause is FU-8 × FU-15 on the client side (`client/src/net_stack.rs`, FU-15 queue drop). This is not yet proven.
- **Next step** (not done): client debug logging for the flow's reset and queue drop, then a decision on the owner.
- **No code was changed.**
- **Tracked as FU-16** in `Sprint22/path.md` (Follow-ups).
